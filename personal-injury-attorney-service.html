Who We Serve

  • At-fault and uninsured drivers, including hit-and-run defendants once you have a verified name.
  • Business defendants, such as trucking companies, rideshare operators, and property owners, with agent-for-service verified first.
  • Witnesses and records custodians: deposition, medical records, and trial subpoenas.
  • Out-of-state parties, including subpoenas domesticated through LA Superior Court.

If the Defendant Avoids Service

  • Skip trace first, so no one drives to an outdated or stale address.
  • Stakeout surveillance at a known home or workplace, serving on contact.
  • Documented diligence and a court-ready skip trace report to support your motion for alternative service, such as service by publication.
