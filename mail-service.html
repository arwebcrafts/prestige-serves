Required Follow-Up Mailing

Substituted Service Mailing under CCP § 415.20

When documents are left with a competent adult or posted at the property, California law requires a follow-up mailing to complete service. You are charged only when substituted or posted service actually occurs. If we serve your party personally, this fee never applies. It is an add-on to a serve and is never billed on its own.

No Field Attempts Needed

Service by Mail with Notice & Acknowledgment under CCP § 415.30

A standalone option for cooperative parties. Your documents are mailed with the Notice & Acknowledgment of Receipt forms and a prepaid return envelope, with no field attempts required. Service is complete when the defendant signs and returns the acknowledgment, and you receive a court-ready proof of service.

Service Rate What's Included Best For
Basic N&A Mail Service $65 Document prep and First-Class mailing with N&A forms and a prepaid return envelope; monitoring for the signed acknowledgment; completed proof of service on return. Cooperative parties, amicable matters, and represented defendants.
N&A Plus $85 Everything in Basic, sent Certified Mail with Return Receipt, plus one follow-up reminder mailing if the acknowledgment is not returned within 20 days. When you want a tracked paper trail or expect the defendant may need a reminder.
N&A with Fallback $150 Everything in N&A Plus. If the defendant never signs, $100 converts to a credit toward standard personal service, so you never pay for service twice. When cooperation is uncertain and you want zero risk of paying twice.
