Mail Service Pricing

Internal & Web Reference · Effective July 2026

All prices include postage, materials, and the declaration of mailing. No postage surcharges, ever. The only exception: document sets over 50 pages add $0.50 per additional page.

Section 1

Substituted Service Mailing — CCP § 415.20

The follow-up mailing required by law after documents are left with a competent adult or posted at the property. Charged only when substituted or posted service actually occurs. This is an add-on to a serve — never sold on its own.

Service Rate What It Includes When to Quote
Standard First-Class Mailing $25 USPS First-Class postage, envelope prep, declaration of mailing added to the proof of service. Default option. Quote this unless the client asks for tracking.
Certified Mail Upgrade $35 Certified Mail with tracking. Everything in Standard, plus a documented paper trail. Quote for evictions/unlawful detainers or when the attorney wants proof of mailing beyond the declaration.

Section 2

Service by Mail with Notice & Acknowledgment — CCP § 415.30

A standalone product for cooperative defendants. No field attempts — documents are mailed with the Notice & Acknowledgment forms and a prepaid return envelope. Service is complete when the defendant signs and returns the acknowledgment.

Service Rate What It Includes When to Quote
Basic N&A Mail Service $65 Document prep and First-Class mailing with N&A forms and prepaid return envelope; monitoring for the signed acknowledgment; completed proof of service on return. Client expects the defendant to cooperate (amicable parties, represented defendants).
N&A Plus $85 Everything in Basic, sent Certified Mail with Return Receipt, plus one follow-up reminder mailing if the acknowledgment is not returned within 20 days. Client wants a tracked paper trail or expects the defendant may need a nudge.
N&A with Fallback $150 Everything in N&A Plus. If the defendant never signs, $100 of the fee converts to a credit toward standard personal service ($50 is retained for the completed mailing work). Best value pitch: removes the client's fear of paying twice. Quote this when the client is hesitant about mail service.

Quick Quoting Guide (For Staff)

  • The § 415.20 mailing ($25/$35) is conditional — tell clients up front: "If we complete service by substitution, a $25 mailing is required by law and will be added." Never bill it if personal service succeeds.
