Frequently Asked Questions

Can you find a renter who has left California?

Yes. Out-of-state renters are common in Los Angeles. We run a skip trace to find a current address, then serve through our nationwide network, with a proof of service for your Los Angeles filing. Service on someone outside the United States follows separate rules, so contact us before filing.

Can you serve small claims cases?

Yes. Small claims has its own service rules, so tell us the case type and the court when you book, and we will confirm the right method for that filing.

Can you recover the vehicle?

No. We do not repossess or recover vehicles. Recovery of a stolen or unreturned vehicle is handled by law enforcement or a licensed repossession agency. Once you have a case and a defendant's name, we locate and serve that person.

What is the lowest-cost way to serve a renter who might cooperate?

For civil filings, service by mail with Notice and Acknowledgment (CCP 415.30) starts at $65 and requires no field attempts. If the renter never signs, our fallback option converts $100 into a credit toward personal service, so you do not pay for service twice.
