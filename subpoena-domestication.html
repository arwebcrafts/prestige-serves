Out-of-State Subpoenas, Made Enforceable

What Is Subpoena Domestication in California?

A subpoena only carries legal force in the state where it was issued. When your case is pending outside California but the witness, records custodian, or corporate registered agent you need is located in Los Angeles, your out-of-state ("foreign") subpoena has no power here until a California court re-issues it. That step is called domestication, and getting it wrong is expensive: rejected paperwork, defective service, blown discovery deadlines, and proofs that don't survive a challenge.

California follows the Uniform Interstate Depositions and Discovery Act (UIDDA). For you, that means one thing: you do not need to hire California counsel or open a new case. You send us your subpoena, and we handle the entire process through the Superior Court of California, County of Los Angeles, then complete service under California law with documented diligence that holds up in court.

The paperwork must be prepared to the court's exacting requirements, presented at the right courthouse, and served in strict compliance with California procedure. That is precisely the work we do every day, minutes from the Stanley Mosk Courthouse.
