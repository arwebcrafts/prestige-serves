Verified Agent Lookup & Diligent Attempting

When the Registered Agent Isn't Where the State Says It Is

Serving a business entity should be simple: locate the registered agent, deliver the documents, and file the proof. In practice, Secretary of State filings lag, agents relocate, and staff are often instructed to deny receipt of legal documents.

We verify the entity’s current agent for service before we knock, cross-checking Secretary of State records against active operating data. Where a filing is outdated, we locate the business’s active headquarters or an officer authorized to accept under CCP § 416.10 — so a stale filing doesn’t stall your litigation.

Secretary of State Backlog?

If state records are delayed, our team independently verifies active corporate filings and operating addresses, delivering timestamped, photo-verified documentation that supports substitute service or due diligence declarations.
